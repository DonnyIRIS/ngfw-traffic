PAN NGFW Traffic Monitor — Support

Live inbound/outbound interface throughput for PAN-OS next-generation firewalls, on your iPhone.

Requirements

  • A firewall running PAN-OS with HTTPS management enabled on the interface you connect to.
  • An admin account with XML API access. A read-only (superuser read-only or a custom role with Operational Requests) is enough.
  • Network access from your iPhone to the management IP: same LAN, or over a VPN such as GlobalProtect.

Troubleshooting

  • Timed out — your iPhone cannot reach the management IP. Check Wi-Fi/VPN and any permitted-IP list on the management profile.
  • Connection refused — HTTPS is not enabled in the interface management profile, or the port is wrong.
  • Certificate changed — the firewall certificate was replaced. Open the firewall in the app and tap Re-check certificate.
  • Invalid credentials — the account lacks XML API permission or the API key was revoked. Use Update credentials.
  • iOS asks to find devices on your local network — allow it; the app needs this to reach firewalls on your LAN.
