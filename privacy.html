Privacy Policy

PAN NGFW Traffic Monitor (“the app”), by Don Coulson. Effective November 8, 2026.

Summary

The app does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data. There are no accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs, and no servers operated by the developer.

What the app stores on your device

Firewall addresses, interface selections, labels and settings are stored locally on your iPhone. API keys for your firewalls are stored in the iOS Keychain on your device and are never sent anywhere except to the firewall they belong to. If you sign in with a username and password, the password is used once to request an API key from your firewall and is not stored.

Network connections

The app connects only to the firewall management addresses you enter, directly from your device over HTTPS, to read interface counters, session counts and temperatures. No data is relayed through any third party.

Purchases

The optional Pro unlock is processed by Apple through the App Store. The developer does not receive your payment information.

Children

The app is a network administration tool and is not directed at children.
